## The Egg Salad You'll Make Every Week

There's a reason egg salad has been a lunchtime staple for generations. When done right — with properly cooked eggs, a balanced dressing, and the right texture — it's one of the most satisfying sandwiches you can make at home.

### The Secret Is in the Slice

The biggest mistake people make with egg salad is roughly chopping eggs with a knife, resulting in uneven, mushy chunks. The game-changer? A **stainless steel egg slicer**. With one smooth press, you get perfectly even slices. Rotate the egg 90 degrees and press again — instant uniform dice, every time. No mess, no effort.

### Perfect Eggs, Every Time

Overcooked eggs turn rubbery and get that grey ring around the yolk. The key is timing: exactly 10 minutes in boiling water, followed immediately by an ice bath. If you make eggs often (and you should), an **electric egg boiler with a timer** does all this automatically — just set it and walk away.

### Make It Your Own

This recipe is a canvas. Add a little hot sauce for heat, swap celery for pickles for tang, or fold in a handful of chopped bacon for richness. Once you master the base, the variations are endless.
