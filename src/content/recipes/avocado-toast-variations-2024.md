## Breakfast, Upgraded

Avocado toast isn't just a trend; it's a reliable, nutrient-dense breakfast that provides healthy fats, complex carbohydrates, and immense satisfaction. But why settle for a plain mash when you can create café-quality masterpieces right in your own kitchen?

### The Foundation of Great Toast

The secret to incredible avocado toast starts with the bread. You need a sturdy foundation to hold the heavy toppings without getting soggy. **Artisan sourdough** is the undisputed king here. Its slight tang beautifully complements the rich, creamy avocado. 

### Mastering the Mash

Never blend your avocado into a smooth puree! The best texture is achieved by roughly mashing the avocado with a fork. The addition of fresh lemon juice prevents oxidation (keeping it bright green) and cuts through the richness, while a splash of high-quality olive oil adds a luxurious mouthfeel.

### Essential Tools

Slicing through a crusty loaf of sourdough requires a high-quality serrated knife, and prepping delicate toppings like ripe cherry tomatoes or wafer-thin red onions is impossible without a razor-sharp chef's knife. If you're baking your own sourdough to use for this toast, a reliable baking mat is an absolute game-changer for getting that perfect crust.
