  - "1 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined"
  - "8 oz linguine or spaghetti"
  - "4 cloves garlic, minced"
  - "1/4 cup dry white wine (optional)"
  - "1 lemon, juiced and zested"
  - "2 tablespoons olive oil"
  - "2 tablespoons unsalted butter"
  - "1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped"
  - "Salt and freshly cracked black pepper"
  - "Red pepper flakes (optional for heat)"
directions:
  - "Prep the ingredients: Mince the garlic, zest and juice the lemon, and chop the parsley."
  - "Cook the pasta: In a large pot of salted boiling water, cook the linguine until al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup of pasta water before draining."
  - "Sauté the shrimp: While pasta cooks, heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add shrimp, season with salt and pepper, and cook for 1 minute per side until pink. Remove and set aside."
  - "Make the sauce: In the same skillet, melt butter. Add minced garlic and red pepper flakes, cooking for 30 seconds until fragrant. Pour in white wine (or a splash of chicken broth) to deglaze the pan. Simmer for 2 minutes."
  - "Combine: Toss the cooked pasta into the skillet. Stir in lemon juice, lemon zest, and a splash of pasta water. Add the shrimp back in and toss everything until well coated."
  - "Garnish and serve: Top with freshly chopped parsley and serve immediately."

## The Ultimate Weeknight Dinner

There’s nothing quite like the combination of bright lemon, pungent garlic, and succulent shrimp. This **One-Pan Lemon Garlic Shrimp Pasta** is our go-to recipe when we want a meal that feels like it came from a high-end Italian restaurant, but requires barely any cleanup.

### Why You'll Love It

The magic of this dish lies in its simplicity. By cooking everything in quick succession—and utilizing a high-quality chef's knife for fast prep—you build incredible layers of flavor in a single skillet. The butter and olive oil create a luxurious base, while the lemon juice cuts through the richness, leaving a bright and zesty finish.

### Pro Tips for Perfect Shrimp

1. **Don't Overcook:** Shrimp cook incredibly fast. The moment they turn opaque and form a "C" shape, they are done. Remove them from the pan immediately to prevent them from becoming rubbery.
2. **Use Fresh Lemon:** Bottled lemon juice lacks the vibrant essential oils found in fresh lemon zest. Always use a fresh lemon for this recipe!
3. **Pasta Water is Gold:** The starch in the reserved pasta water helps emulsify the butter and lemon juice, creating a silky sauce that clings beautifully to the linguine.

### Preparing Your Kitchen

To make this recipe as seamless as possible, we highly recommend having your ingredients prepped *before* you start cooking (mise en place). A sharp chef's knife is absolutely essential for finely mincing the garlic and effortlessly chopping the parsley.
