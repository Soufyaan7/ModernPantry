  - "Add garlic and spices: Stir in the minced garlic, cumin, and smoked paprika. Cook for 1 more minute until fragrant."
  - "Simmer: Add the rinsed lentils, diced tomatoes (with juices), and vegetable broth. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 35-40 minutes, or until the lentils are tender."
  - "Blend (optional): For a creamier texture, use an immersion blender to partially blend the soup, leaving some lentils whole for texture."
  - "Wilt the greens: Stir in the fresh baby spinach and let it cook for 2 minutes until wilted."

## The Ultimate Comfort Food

When the weather turns chilly, there is absolutely nothing better than a steaming bowl of soup. This **Hearty Vegan Lentil Soup** is a nutritional powerhouse, packed with plant-based protein, fiber, and essential vitamins. Better yet, it tastes incredible and only requires one pot!

### Building Flavor

The secret to a truly spectacular soup is building flavor in layers. We start with a classic mirepoix (onion, carrots, and celery) sautéed slowly to bring out their natural sweetness. The addition of smoked paprika gives the broth a deep, savory, almost meaty flavor without using any animal products.

### Why Lentils?

Lentils are one of the most versatile legumes. They don't require overnight soaking, making them perfect for a quick, impromptu dinner. Brown and green lentils hold their shape well during long simmers, providing a satisfying, hearty texture. 

### Perfecting the Temperature

While cooking soups, maintaining the right simmer is crucial to ensuring the lentils cook evenly without turning into mush. If you are serving this alongside roasted vegetables or a vegan roast, a reliable digital thermometer ensures your side dishes hit the exact temperature for maximum flavor.
