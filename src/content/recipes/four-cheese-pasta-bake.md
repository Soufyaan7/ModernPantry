  - "3 tablespoons all-purpose flour"

  - "1/2 teaspoon ground nutmeg"
  - "Salt and white pepper to taste"

directions:
  - "Cook the pasta: Boil pasta in heavily salted water until 2 minutes under al dente. Drain and set aside — it will finish cooking in the oven."
  - "Grate the cheeses: Use your 3-in-1 rotary cheese grater to freshly grate the parmesan, gruyère, and cheddar. Freshly grated cheese melts far better than pre-packaged shreds — this step makes a real difference."
  - "Make the béchamel: Melt butter in a saucepan over medium heat. Whisk in flour and cook 1 minute. Gradually pour in warm milk, whisking constantly until smooth and thick, about 5 minutes."
  - "Add the cheese: Remove from heat and stir in 3/4 of the grated cheeses. Season with nutmeg, salt, and pepper."
  - "Combine: Fold the pasta into the cheese sauce. Pour into a buttered baking dish."
  - "Top and bake: Scatter remaining cheese, mozzarella, and breadcrumbs on top. Bake at 200°C (390°F) for 20-25 minutes until golden and bubbling."
  - "Rest and serve: Let sit for 5 minutes before serving. Garnish with fresh parsley."

## The Pasta Bake That Beats Any Restaurant Version

There are pasta bakes, and then there are *four-cheese* pasta bakes. This is the dish you make when you want to genuinely impress — the kind that gets people asking for the recipe before they've even finished their first serving.

### Fresh Grated Cheese Changes Everything

Here's the truth that cheese-lovers know: pre-shredded cheese from a bag contains anti-caking agents (usually cellulose) that prevent it from melting smoothly. The result? Grainy, oily sauce. The solution is always freshly grated cheese — and with a **rotary cheese grater**, it takes about 60 seconds per block. No mess, no sore arms, just perfectly grated cheese that melts into velvet.

### The Four Cheese Philosophy

Each cheese plays a role. **Parmesan** brings sharpness and depth. **Gruyère** adds nutty complexity. **Sharp cheddar** gives bold, familiar flavor. **Mozzarella** creates those irresistible stretchy pulls. Together, they create a sauce that's impossibly rich and perfectly balanced.

### The Béchamel Is Non-Negotiable

Don't shortcut with cream — a proper béchamel (butter + flour + milk) is what gives this dish its silky, clingy consistency. The trick is warm milk and constant whisking. Give it 5 minutes of attention and it will reward you with the best cheese sauce of your life.
